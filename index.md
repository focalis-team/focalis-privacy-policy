# Focalis Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

Focalis (“the app”) is a focus test built on the Schulte table. This Privacy Policy explains what information Focalis collects, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. By using Focalis you agree to this policy.

Questions or requests: focalis.support@gmail.com

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account information.** You sign in with Google. Google shares your email address with us, and may share your name, a link to your profile picture and a Google account identifier. We use your email only to identify your account. We do not show your email, name or picture to other users. We also create a random user ID for your account.
- **Username.** The name you choose when you first sign in. It is visible to other users.
- **Test data.** For every test you complete we store the completion time, the number of mistakes, and the date and time. For a test you start but do not finish we store only a start time. From this data we calculate your best time, averages, trend, rank and percentile.
- **Settings.** Whether your profile is public or private.
- **Technical data.** Our backend provider processes standard technical data, such as IP address and request details, in server logs for security, abuse prevention and reliability. We do not use it for advertising.
- **On your device.** The app keeps your sign-in session and a saved copy of your latest stats and leaderboard results so screens load quickly. This stays on your device and is removed when you log out, delete your account or uninstall the app.

**What we do not collect:** your location, contacts, photos, videos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, payment information or health data.

## 2. How we use information

- To create and secure your account and sign you in.
- To record your test results and calculate your statistics, rankings and leaderboards.
- To check results against our server clock, which keeps rankings fair and helps detect cheating.
- To show your public profile and let other users find you by username.
- To operate, maintain and secure the service, prevent abuse and respond to your support requests.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## 3. What other users can see

By default your profile is public. Other signed-in Focalis users can see your username, best time, averages, number of tests, rank and percentile, your recent results as a trend chart, and your place on the leaderboards. Your email address is never shown to other users.

You can make your profile private at any time in Settings. A private profile hides your stats and removes you from the leaderboards. Your username may still appear in player search results, but your stats are not shown.

## 4. Who we share information with

- **Supabase** provides our authentication and database hosting. It processes your data on our behalf and only to run the service.
- **Google** provides Google Sign-In. Google’s own privacy policy applies to the sign-in process.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the service.
- **Business changes.** If Focalis is transferred to another owner, your information may be transferred with it.

Focalis does not contain advertising networks or third-party analytics, and we do not share data with data brokers.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

- We keep your account and test data for as long as your account exists.
- **Delete in the app:** go to Settings, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, results and statistics from our live database.
- **Delete by email:** write to focalis.support@gmail.com from the email address of your Google account. See also the account deletion page for this app.
- Unfinished test records and older individual results may be removed automatically from time to time. Summary statistics are kept while your account exists.
- We may keep backups for a limited period. Backups are not used to restore deleted accounts to active use, and deleted data leaves them when they are rotated.
- The saved copy on your device is removed when you log out, delete your account or uninstall the app.

## 6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access is restricted by rules that let each user reach only the data they are permitted to see, and test results are written only through server-side checks. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. You can delete your account in the app at any time, make your profile private in Settings, or contact us at focalis.support@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

## 8. Children and teens

Focalis is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete the account and its data. If you believe a child under 13 is using Focalis, please contact us at focalis.support@gmail.com.

If you are under 18, or under the age at which you can consent to the processing of your data where you live, please use Focalis only with the permission of a parent or guardian. Parents and guardians can contact us at focalis.support@gmail.com to ask us to review, correct or delete their child’s data, and we will respond within 30 days.

We recommend that younger users make their profile private in Settings and choose a username that does not reveal their real name.

## 9. International processing

Our service providers may process data on servers in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 10. Not a medical tool

Focalis is a general measure of visual attention speed. It is not a medical or diagnostic tool. We do not use your results to diagnose or treat any condition, and we do not treat them as health records.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If we make significant changes, we will tell you in the app or on the store listing.
