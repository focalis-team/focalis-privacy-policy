# Focalis: Train Your Focus — Account Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your Focalis account and associated data.

## How to delete your account

If you can access the Focalis mobile app:

1. Open Focalis: Train Your Focus.
2. Sign in using your Google account.
3. Open your Profile or Settings screen.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.
5. Follow the instructions to confirm deletion.

Your account deletion request will be processed through the app's account deletion functionality.

## Cannot access the app?

If you cannot access your Focalis account or delete it through the app, you can request account deletion by email.

Send an email from the email address associated with your Focalis account to [**focalis.support@gmail.com**](mailto\:focalis.support@gmail.com).

## What data is deleted?

When your account is deleted, Focalis will delete the account information and associated data that its deletion process covers, such as:

- Your Focalis profile information.
- Your saved game scores and performance history.
- Other personal information associated with your account, where applicable.
